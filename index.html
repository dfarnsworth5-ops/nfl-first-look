DVP
Defense vs. Position rank — how favorable the matchup is for that position group. Blue/green = favorable matchup for the offense.
SA DVP
Schedule-Adjusted DVP — the same Defense vs. Position rank, but adjusted for the quality of the offenses each defense has faced. It is calculated from the 2026 schedule plus fantasy points scored (offense) and allowed (defense) by position, solving for offense and defense strength together. A defense that gave up big numbers mostly to elite offenses ranks tougher than its raw DVP; one that padded its stats against weak offenses ranks easier. Ratings are pulled partway toward average to account for the small early-season sample. 1 = toughest matchup, 32 = easiest — same direction as DVP, so compare the two to spot defenses the raw rank is fooling you on.

Top 5 Report

Matchup Score
The average of the matchup ranks shown (QB and RB: O EPA, DVP and SA DVP; WR and TE: DVP and SA DVP). 32 = best possible matchup for the offense. RB, WR and TE matchups are team-level, so the lead RB (and top two WRs) on each team are listed.
Regression (Buy-Low)
xFPPG minus FPPG. A positive gap means the player's usage and opportunity say he should be scoring more than he has. Players below a minimum FPPG are left out to keep out part-timers.
Pressure Score
D/ST only. The average percentile of the defense's own pressure rate and the opposing offensive line's pressure rate allowed, scaled 0-100.
SA DVP Gap
SA DVP minus DVP. A big positive gap means the matchup is easier than its raw rank suggests; a big negative gap means it is harder.

Color Coding

Every colored stat is ranked against the rest of that day's slate and shaded on a continuous scale — deep blue for the best matchups/performances, deep orange/red for the worst, with lighter shades in between. The scale is calculated automatically from that day's data, so it always reflects the current slate rather than a fixed cutoff.
